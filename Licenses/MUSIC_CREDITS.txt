DIAMOND QUEST — SAMPLE INSTRUMENT AND SCORE CREDITS

The active expedition-thriller score is produced by Production/compose_action_thriller.py,
combining original procedural bass and percussion with sampled strings and brass.
The new original compositions and stings are described by the included Python,
MIDI and event sources in Production/. The game plays pre-rendered Ogg/WAV files.
