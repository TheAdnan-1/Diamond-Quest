DIAMOND QUEST — EXPEDITION EDITION

1. Extract the entire ZIP. Keep main.py and the diamondquest folder together.
2. On Windows, double-click "Play Diamond Quest.bat".
   Python 3.9 or newer must be installed, with Python added to PATH.
   The launcher installs pygame and numpy if needed.
3. Choose BEGIN EXPEDITION, then START MATCH.

Other systems:
  python3 -m pip install -r requirements.txt
  python3 main.py

Controls:
  WASD or arrows    Move
  H / P / K        Hammer / push / hook, then press a direction
  E                Place a mine, then choose a direction
  Space            Wait
  C                Switch between player, AI, and shared cameras
  G                Toggle cell boundaries and movement-count overlay
  M                Large map
  Tab              Show/hide minimap
  Esc              Pause

The first launch prepares and caches the artwork. Music and effects are pre-rendered.
All image assets are included. No image-generation service or API key is needed.
Game rules, scores, costs, damage, maps and AI are preserved.
All 34 original sound-effect categories have replacement designs in this update.
New: 190 sound clips, three adaptive orchestral themes, HD replacement characters,
42-frame snake strikes, a full burn mantle, and slumped/recovering stun animations.
You control the blue-gi boy with the S badge. The red-haired girl with the A badge
is the AI rival. Her front-facing poses look straight forward. Neither carries
a torch or staff.
Frost Cavern: smooth blue-gray paths are open, granular gray-brown ground is
diggable, and dark raised rock blocks are solid. The M map includes a terrain legend.
The background music uses driving 126-138 BPM action-thriller themes with drums,
bass, rapid strings, melodic hooks and builds. The sound effects are unchanged.
A music-only sampler is included in Previews. Restart the game after updating.
Hover a cell to see walking moves and a numbered route. Hazards may add delays.

Full rules and technical details are in README.md.
